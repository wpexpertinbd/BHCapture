BHCapture is installed 🎉

BHCapture lives in the menu bar. Press ⌃⌘3 for All-in-One, ⌃⌘4 for a screenshot, ⌃⌘5 to record.

First capture: macOS asks for Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Allow it, then quit and reopen BHCapture — a running app never sees a new permission. Recording your voice asks for Microphone permission the first time.

Free & open-source: github.com/wpexpertinbd/BHCapture — thank you for using BHCapture!
