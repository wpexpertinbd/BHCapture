BHCapture __VERSION__

A free screenshot and screen recorder — select any area, annotate it, record it with your voice.

  • All-in-One capture: Image or Video from one shortcut (⌃⌘3).
  • An editor with arrows, shapes, text, steps and pixelate.
  • MP4 recording with system sound and a noise-filtered microphone.
  • Nothing leaves your Mac: no account, no upload.

This installer puts BHCapture.app in your Applications folder. If an older copy is running it is quit first, and the new one opens when the install finishes.
